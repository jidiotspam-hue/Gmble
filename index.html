How it works

      
    
        
  • Start with §500 sonnetous. Bet it on anything. Whole numbers only, minimum bet §1.
    • 
        
  • Going broke: if you have under §1 and no open bets, you're broke. From the next calendar day you can claim a §100 bailout, but it costs you: a 25% winnings tax for 3 days (a quarter of the profit on every winning bet is confiscated) and a 💀 on the leaderboard.
    • 
        
  • Fixed odds: the odds (like ×3.00) are locked when you bet. Payout = stake × odds, rounded down, and it includes your stake.
    • 
        
  • Pool: everyone's stakes go into one pot; winners split the whole pot pro-rata. Odds shown are live (total pool ÷ that option's pool) and change as bets arrive. If nobody backed the winner, everyone is refunded.
    • 
        
  • ⏱ Timer markets ask “How long till…?”. Pick a bucket: within 1 day (×6), 1–4 days (×3), 4–8 days (×1.8) or 8+ days (×1.3). When it happens, anyone can report it (house markets) with the time it happened; the matching bucket wins. No report after 8 days? It settles as 8+.
    • 
        
  • ☑ Choice markets have 2–6 options. House ones have fixed odds; ones made by players are pools and only the creator can settle or void them.
    • 
        
  • Daily house markets: 4 fresh markets appear every day (UTC), the same for everyone. Betting closes when the timer runs out; then it's “awaiting result”.
    • 
        
  • Net worth = balance + stakes in open bets. The leaderboard ranks by it.
